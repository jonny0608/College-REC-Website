ABOUT THE SCHOOL

The Rowan Experience

Rowan University is located in Glassboro, New Jersey. Rowan is a public university that offers a wide range of academic programs and hands-on learning opportunities. Students can explore their interests, engage in research, and participate in a vibrant campus community.

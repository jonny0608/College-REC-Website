ABOUT THE SCHOOL

The Rowan Experience

Rowan University is a public research university in Glassboro, New Jersey, offering academics, research, hands-on learning, organizations, and career opportunities.

A Campus Built for Possibility

Rowan combines the opportunities of a growing public research university with a campus community where students can become involved in academics, research, and organizations.

Projects, internships, leadership opportunities, and research help students connect their education with future careers.

LOCATION Glassboro, New Jersey
SCHOOL TYPE Public Research University
WHAT STANDS OUT Hands-on learning + campus community
